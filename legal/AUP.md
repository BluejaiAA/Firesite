# Firesite — Acceptable Use Policy

> **STATUS: DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
> For solicitor review before publication.

By using Firesite you agree not to:

## 1. Misuse the service

- Use Firesite to produce a fire risk assessment for premises where you
  do not meet the competence requirements of Article 18 of the
  Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 (as amended) or any
  successor legislation.
- Misrepresent the source, accuracy or authorship of any assessment.
- Knowingly enter false information into an assessment.
- Use the service to produce assessments for buildings within scope of
  Part 4 of the Building Safety Act 2022 (Higher-Risk Buildings) **if
  Firesite has not been certified for that use**. Check the latest
  product documentation before relying on Firesite for HRB work.

## 2. Misuse other people's data

- Process Personal Data through Firesite for purposes other than
  recording the fire risk assessment in question.
- Upload special-category data (health, biometric, etc.) unless you have
  identified a lawful basis under UK GDPR Article 9.
- Photograph identifiable individuals without their consent except where
  necessary for the FRA and lawful under UK GDPR.

## 3. Attack the service

- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the service except under a
  documented coordinated-disclosure programme published by Bluejai.
- Circumvent rate limits, authentication, authorisation, or quotas.
- Reverse-engineer or decompile the service beyond what is permitted by
  applicable law.
- Use bots, scrapers, or automated agents to extract data from the
  service in bulk without a written agreement.
- Submit malicious code, malware, or attempt to exploit any
  vulnerability you become aware of.

## 4. Compete unfairly

- Use Firesite to build a competing product (e.g. by training a model on
  its question set, report templates, or guidance content).
- Resell access to Firesite without an authorised reseller agreement.

## 5. Be a bad citizen

- Use the service in violation of UK law, including the Computer Misuse
  Act 1990, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Equality Act 2010, the
  Bribery Act 2010, or applicable sanctions and export-control rules.
- Use the service to harass, defame, or discriminate against any
  individual or group.

---

## Enforcement

Suspected breaches should be reported to **[abuse@bluejaisafety.example]**.
We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that breach this
policy, with or without notice depending on severity. We will cooperate
with law enforcement and regulators where required.
