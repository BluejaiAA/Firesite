FAQ

Common questions

Does FireSite work without an internet connection? +
Yes. FireSite is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works fully offline once installed. Start assessments in basements, plant rooms or anywhere without signal. Your data syncs to the cloud automatically when you reconnect.
Is FireSite compliant with PAS 79:2020 and the RRO? +
FireSite's 163 questions were designed by chartered fire engineers and align with PAS 79:2020 guidance and the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005. Responsibility for the fire risk assessment remains with the assessor, but the tool helps ensure nothing is overlooked.
How do I export my assessment as a PDF report? +
Once you complete an assessment, navigate to the Report tab. Tap the Share button to open your device's native share sheet — you can email the report, save it to files, or print it directly from your phone or tablet. PDF export is available on Starter, Professional and Enterprise plans.
Can I use FireSite on iPhone or Android? +
Yes. FireSite is a PWA (Progressive Web App) that runs in your browser on any device — iPhone, Android, iPad or desktop. On iPhone, go to firesite-weld.vercel.app in Safari, tap Share and choose "Add to Home Screen" to install it as an app. On Android, Chrome will prompt you to install it automatically.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time? +
Absolutely. There are no contracts or lock-ins. Cancel any time from your account settings. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period, and your assessment data will be available to download before the account closes.
Is there a team or multi-user option? +
Yes. The Professional plan includes up to 5 team members, and the Enterprise plan supports unlimited users across multiple organisations. Each user can carry out their own assessments while sharing a central dashboard with your team.
