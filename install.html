iPhone & iPad
Using Safari
1
Open Safari and go to the link above (or scan the QR code)

Android

1
Open Chrome and go to the link above (or scan the QR code)
2
Tap the three dots menu in the top-right corner ⋮
3
Tap "Add to Home screen"
4
Tap "Add" to confirm — the icon appears instantly! 🔥
